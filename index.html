Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about escrow or AI matches? We have answers.

How does escrow protection keep payouts secure?

When a brand launches a campaign, funds are locked in our secure escrow. Creators submit their completed drafts, which are audited for compliance. Payouts release instantly to creators only after the brand approves the final deliverables.

What is the AI Content Review?

Our built-in AI audits drafts automatically. It checks if the brand logo is clearly visible, verifies that required hashtags like #ad are included in captions, and detects copyright or profanity risks before anything is published.

Can creators join and use CR8 for free?

Yes! Creators can sign up, list their accounts, and search opportunities for free. We only charge a small 5% transaction commission on free tier payouts.

How does the creator matching work?

Instead of simple tag filters, our system calculates a fit score using follower demographics, engagement rates, brand affinities, and fake follower risk audits to suggest the perfect candidates.
